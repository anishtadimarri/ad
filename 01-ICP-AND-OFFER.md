# ICP and Main Offer

## The ICP, stated precisely

> Dental practices doing **full-arch implant treatment** (All-on-4 / All-on-X), **$2–8M annual
> revenue**, 1–3 locations, **owner-dentist is the decision maker**, already spending
> **$10,000+/month on patient acquisition**, generating **15+ full-arch consults per month**,
> with a treatment coordinator or a designated person who runs consults.

### Qualification criteria (all must be true)

| Criterion | Threshold | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Full-arch consults/month | **15+** | Below this there isn't enough volume for the guarantee to be deliverable |
| Average full-arch case value | **$25,000+** | Below this our fee isn't a rounding error |
| Monthly marketing spend | **$10,000+** | Proves they buy solutions and have lead flow to work |
| Decision maker on the call | **Owner-dentist** | Associates and office managers can't authorize $9,500 |
| Has a CRM or lead management tool | Any | We need somewhere to live that isn't the PMS |
| Has a designated consult person | Any | Someone must work the call tasks we assign |

### Hard disqualifiers

- **Under 15 consults/month** — guarantee is undeliverable, walk away
- **Insurance/Medicaid-dependent full-arch** — margin too thin, financing irrelevant
- **Everything locked in Dentrix with no external access and no CRM** — delivery cost explodes
- **DSO-owned or multi-location corporate** — procurement cycle, wrong buyer, revisit in year 2
- **Practice in a growth-hostile state for our stack** — check lender availability first
- **Owner won't get on camera** — Module 1 requires a pre-frame video. No video, no Module 1

### Where they are findable

1. **Meta Ad Library** — search "full arch," "All-on-4," "dental implants" and filter by US
   advertisers running >60 days. These are pre-qualified: they spend, they're pained, they're
   active. This is the primary list source.
2. AAID and Academy of Osseointegration member directories
3. Google Maps + "dental implant center" in top 50 metros
4. Implant CE course alumni (Nobel, Straumann, ZimVie training programs) — dentists who just
   paid $25–50k to learn full-arch and can't close cases yet

## The Main Offer

### Name

**The Lead-to-Chair Revenue System**

### One-sentence positioning

> "We put more pre-approved full-arch patients in your chair, and we work the ones who don't
> close. We never touch your marketing and we never coach your team."

### What's included

| Module | Deliverable | Outcome metric |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Speed + Show** | Sub-60-second lead response, qualification logic, booking, 4-touch confirmation cadence, pre-frame video, no-show recovery | **No-show 35% → 15%** |
| **2. Pre-Approved Arrival** | 5-lender stack, credit-tier waterfall routing, pre-qual link in confirmation sequence, decline recovery paths, payment-first presentation scripts | **60% arrive pre-approved; 30–40% of declines recovered** |
| **3. Long-Cycle Recovery** | 6-month automated nurture on every non-closer, human call tasks assigned into their system, recent-consult recovery (last 90–180 days) | **1–2 recovered cases/month** |
| **Reporting** | Weekly scorecard: consults booked, sat, sat-pre-approved, by source; approval rate by lender by tier | Baseline vs. current |

Full deliverable specs in [`04-PRODUCTS.md`](04-PRODUCTS.md).

### Explicitly out of scope — say this on the sales call

- ❌ We don't run or touch your ads. Your agency keeps their job.
- ❌ We don't coach your treatment coordinator or review consult recordings.
- ❌ We don't touch your practice management system or clinical records.
- ❌ We don't contact patients as you — every message goes out under your practice name from
  your systems.

Saying what you *don't* do is the single strongest trust move available to an unknown vendor.
It also removes the biggest objection in the category: *"I just signed a 12-month agency contract."*

## Pricing

### Standard structure

| Component | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Install** | **$9,500** | 50% at signature, 50% at go-live (day 21) |
| **Management** | **$2,500/month** | Monthly, starting at go-live |
| **Performance** | **$300 per consult sat above baseline** | Monthly, in arrears |

**Baseline** = average monthly consults *sat* over the trailing 90 days, agreed in writing
before go-live. This number is the single most important line in the contract. Get it from
their own records, in writing, signed.

### Why this structure

- **High install** funds CAC and gets the 30-day payback the model needs
- **Low base** makes renewal easy and churn low
- **Performance on consults sat** is the innovation — it's countable, undisputable, and it
  means you get paid more precisely when you're working. You could never price this way on
  "case acceptance rate"

### Annual prepay option (offer at signature)

**12 months for the price of 10** → $9,500 install + $25,000 prepaid = **$34,500 day one.**
Performance fees still bill monthly. Expect ~15% take rate from cold clients with no track
record. Do not discount further to get it.

### Founding client pricing (first 3 clients only)

$4,500 install + $1,500/month + $300/consult, in exchange for:
- Filmed video testimonial once results land
- Named case study with real numbers
- Two warm referrals

Do not extend past client three. Raise to standard immediately after.

## The Guarantee

> **We will increase your consults sat by at least 5 per month within 90 days, or we work
> free until we do.**
>
> Conditional on: you deliver at least 15 full-arch consults booked per month, your designated
> consult person works the call tasks we assign within 48 hours, and you complete the pre-frame
> video within the first 14 days.

### Why the conditions matter

The guarantee is hostage to two inputs you don't control: their lead flow and their staff.
State the conditions out loud on the sales call — it reads as competence, not weaseling, and
it's the only thing standing between you and working free for a client whose agency had a bad
quarter.

### Guarantee math

At 25 consults booked and 35% no-show, 16 sit. Getting to 15% no-show means 21 sit. That's
**+5, which is exactly the guarantee** — so you're promising the no-show fix alone and
treating financing recovery and nurture as upside. Deliberately conservative.

## Contract terms to get right

| Term | Position |
|---|---|
| Initial term | 6 months, then month-to-month |
| Cancellation | 30 days written notice after initial term |
| Baseline consults sat | **Agreed in writing pre-launch. Non-negotiable.** |
| BAA | Signed before any system access |
| Offshore disclosure | Named in BAA; client acknowledges India-based delivery team |
| Sending identity | All patient messaging goes out as the practice, from practice-owned systems |
| Consent warranty | **Client represents and warrants it holds valid consent for all contacts, and indemnifies us** |
| Data ownership | Client owns all patient data. We retain aggregate performance data |
| IP | We retain the system, sequences, and scripts. Client gets a license for the term |

That consent warranty clause is the most important sentence in the contract. Do not sign
without it.
