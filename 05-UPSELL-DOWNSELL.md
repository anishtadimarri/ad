# Upsells

## U1 — Annual Prepay (offer at signature, same call)

**12 months for the price of 10.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Standard | $9,500 install + $2,500/mo |
| **Prepay** | **$9,500 install + $25,000 prepaid = $34,500 day one** |
| Their saving | $5,000 |
| Performance fees | Still billed monthly on top |

**When to offer:** immediately after they say yes to the main offer, before the paperwork. Never
lead with it — it reads as desperate and it discounts a sale you already made.

**Script:**
> "One option before I send the agreement. If you'd rather not think about a monthly invoice,
> you can prepay twelve months for the price of ten — that's $25,000 instead of $30,000. Some
> practices prefer it for the tax treatment and some don't want another recurring line item.
> Either is fine, I just don't want you to find out later that it existed."

**Expected take rate:** ~15% from cold clients with no track record. 30–40% once you have case
studies. **Do not discount further to force it** — the standard structure already clears your
payback target.

**Impact:** a prepay client delivers $34,500 in 30 days versus $12,750. At a $1,428 CAC that's
**19:1**, and it front-loads the cash that funds your ad scaling.

## U2 — Consult Volume (month 4+) ← the real money

Once the funnel converts, sell the traffic.

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | **$6,000/month** + client-funded ad spend |
| Trigger | 90 days live, guarantee met, consults-sat baseline beaten |
| What it is | You now run their full-arch patient acquisition |

**Why this is the most important rung in the ladder:** it is the one upsell nobody else in the
market can make, because you're the only vendor who can say the thing that makes it obvious:

> "Your consults now show up at 85% and 6 in 10 arrive already approved for $38,000. Every lead
> you buy is worth roughly 40% more than it was in January. That's exactly when it makes sense
> to buy more of them — and I'd rather it be me than someone who doesn't know how your funnel
> works."

**Note the tension with your positioning.** You launched on "we don't touch your marketing." So
frame U2 as an invitation the client earns, never as a pitch you lead with, and never propose it
before day 90. If they're happy with their agency, drop it permanently and sell U3 instead.

**Impact on ARPU:** $2,500 → $8,500/month. Nearly 3.5x, on a client who already trusts you.

## U3 — Second-Arch Sequence

Most full-arch patients need both arches but finance one. Nobody systematically follows up for
the second.

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | **$1,500 install + $500 per second-arch case closed** |
| What it is | 12-month post-treatment sequence: healing check-ins, then the second-arch conversation timed to when their first financing is substantially paid down |
| Their value | $25,000–35,000 per second arch, from a patient who already trusts them |

Highest-margin, lowest-effort upsell in the ladder. Build once, deploy to every client.

## U4 — Multi-Location Rollout

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | **$4,000/month per additional location** (no additional install) |
| Trigger | Client has 2+ locations, or opens one |

Marginal delivery cost is close to zero — the system already exists. This is where ARPU
compounds without new acquisition.

## Upsell sequencing rule

**One at a time, in order, with 90 days between.** Stacking upsells reads as extraction and it's
the fastest way to lose a client who was otherwise happy.

| Client month | Offer |
|---|---|
| 0 | U1 (prepay) at signature only |
| 4 | U3 (second-arch) — small, easy yes, builds the upsell habit |
| 7 | U2 (consult volume) — the big one, only if U3 landed and results are documented |
| 10+ | U4 (multi-location) if applicable |

# Downsells

Triggered when the main offer is declined on the review call. **Never lead with these** — offer
only after a real close attempt and one objection handled.

## D1 — Financing Waterfall Install — $2,500 one-time

| | |
|---|---|
| What they get | All 5 lender applications submitted, waterfall routing built, pre-qual links placed, 4 presentation scripts, approval tracking |
| Delivery | 7 days |
| Margin | ~85% |

**The best downsell in the ladder**, because it's the highest-dollar module and its result shows
up in 2–3 weeks. A practice that recovers two declined patients in month one has a $70,000 reason
to buy the rest.

**Script:**
> "Understood — the full system is a real commitment and I don't want to talk you into it. Let me
> offer something smaller. The single biggest number on your audit was financing declines —
> $[X] a month. I'll install just that piece for $2,500, one time, done in a week. If it works
> the way it works everywhere else, we'll talk about the rest. If it doesn't, you've spent $2,500
> and you keep the lender stack forever."

**Expected conversion to main offer within 60 days: 40–50%.** This is a sequencing tool disguised
as a downsell.

## D2 — Speed + Show Only — $3,500 install + $1,000/month

For the practice whose stated pain is no-shows specifically and who won't buy a three-module
system.

| | |
|---|---|
| What they get | Module 1 complete. No financing waterfall, no long-cycle nurture |
| Guarantee | No-show rate below 20% in 60 days or refund the install |

Naturally upgrades — once no-shows are fixed, the financing leak becomes the obvious next number
on the report.

## D3 — Install Payment Plan — 3 × $3,500

Same full system, install split across 3 months. Total $10,500 (a $1,000 premium for the terms).

| | |
|---|---|
| 30-day collected | $3,500 + $2,500 = **$6,000** |
| GP @ 80% | $4,800 |
| **Ratio at $1,428 CAC** | **3.4:1** |

**Still clears 2:1, but it nearly halves your payback.** Use sparingly — cash-constrained
practices are also the ones most likely to churn. Require the first payment before install begins,
and never offer this before D1.

## D4 — Stay on the list

If nothing closes: they keep the audit and the Blueprint, and they go into a monthly nurture
sequence. Send the aggregate benchmark data — *"the 40 practices we've now audited average 14.2
of 24 on the scorecard; you scored 11"* — which gets sharper every month and costs you nothing.

Re-offer at 60 and 120 days. Roughly 15% of audit buyers who don't close initially will close
within 6 months.

# Revenue per client, modeled

| Path | 30-day | Month 12 cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| Main only | $12,750 | ~$47,000 |
| Main + U1 prepay | $34,500 | ~$41,000 |
| Main + U3 | $12,750 | ~$52,000 |
| **Main + U3 + U2** | $12,750 | **~$88,000** |
| D1 → main at day 60 | $2,500 | ~$40,000 |
| D2 only | $6,000 | ~$18,500 |

**The U2 path is worth nearly 2x the base path.** Which means the single highest-leverage activity
after install isn't finding new clients — it's making the first 90 days work well enough that
month 7 becomes a conversation about traffic.
