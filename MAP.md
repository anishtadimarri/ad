# THE MAP — 2 Verticals × 5 Roles

The decision, after every lens. Supersedes the vertical and role selections in
[`BEACHHEAD.md`](BEACHHEAD.md) and [`TARGETING.md`](TARGETING.md), which contain the reasoning.

## Why two verticals, not three

Six independent screens were run. Only two verticals appear at the top of all of them:

| Screen | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vertical criteria (FIRST-PRINCIPLES §1) | E-comm | MSP/IT | Marketing agency |
| Intent flow (TARGETING §5) | **E-comm** | **Marketing agency** | Freight |
| Meta workability (§5B) | **E-comm** | **Marketing agency** | PI law |
| **Facebook + Instagram both (§5C)** | **E-comm 5/5** | **Marketing agency 5/5** | *nothing else clears* |
| Switch cost / shared ledger (BEACHHEAD) | E-comm | Marketing agency | SaaS |
| Supply-side shortage (PATTERN) | Accounting | — | — |

> **Requiring Facebook *and* Instagram is what forces two rather than three.** Every other candidate
> is Facebook-only — insurance is FB 4 / IG 1, PI law FB 4 / IG 2, freight FB 4 / IG 2. Only e-comm
> founders and agency owners (both 25–45) live on both platforms.

**Do not force a third.** The third slot is a later expansion decision, and when it comes it should
be **PI law firms on Facebook-only placements** — 3rd on Meta workability, `AFFORD` = 5 (the richest
buyer in the set), owner-led. Not at launch.

### Read it as three moves, not ten cells

| Move | Cell | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Launch** | **E-comm × Bookkeeper** | Highest pair score in the entire study (91.2) and the highest Meta-sellability of any role (96.0). Most legible role, thickest job-posting signal, deepest Indian supply |
| **2. Second seat** | **E-comm × AP, then AR** | Zero and 0.5-week switch cost. Sell into a client you already have — seats-per-client is what makes CAC efficient |
| **3. Second vertical** | **Agency × AR, then Ads manager** | Same ledger, same bench, one week of retraining. AR is the agency's #1 felt pain; ads manager is where the founder's own skill becomes unfair advantage |

**Accountant is not a separate campaign** — it is the tier-up you discover on the intake call when
the buyer needs a full close rather than reconciliation. Same ad, 41% higher fee.

## What you actually advertise

**Two ads. Not five.**

| Vertical | The one line | Placements |
|---|---|---|
| **E-comm / DTC** | *"Your bookkeeper costs $75k. Ours is $20k and already knows Shopify + A2X."* | FB + IG, unrestricted |
| **Marketing agency** | *"Your margin is people. Move the back office, keep the creatives."* | FB + IG, unrestricted |

**The role you advertise ≠ the role you place.** "Bookkeeper" is the legible entry; the intake call
decides which of the five seats actually fits:

| They say | You place | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| "My books are a mess" | Bookkeeper | $6.6k |
| "I need month-end closed properly" | **Accountant** | **$9.3k** |
| "It's the unpaid invoices" | AR & Collections | $6.0k |
| "I'm drowning in bills to code" | AP & Invoice Processing | $5.4k |
| "My ad account is a disaster" | Ads manager | $7.2k |

**One legible promise, five sellable seats behind it.** This is also what keeps the bench pooled —
the ad is not committed to the least-portable role.

### Creative

Screen-recorded **graded work sample** as the primary asset — a broken reconciliation being fixed,
scored on camera. It is the only role scoring 5 on both legibility and showability, nobody else in
the category can produce it, and Reels is exactly the format for it.

## What was deliberately left out, and why

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Third vertical at launch** | Nothing else clears FB **and** IG. Forcing one adds a second outbound motion for a worse buyer |
| **SaaS** | Was in BEACHHEAD's trio. Fails Meta at 54.2 — youngest buyer, least Facebook-reachable, and you cannot identify a SaaS company from a job posting |
| **Staffing agencies, MSP/IT** | `FB_NATIVE` = 2. Real buyers, wrong channel. Reachable by cold email, not by Meta |
| **Insurance** | Best supply-side shortage of any occupation (PATTERN §3) and the best single ad line — but FB 4 / **IG 1**. Facebook-only, so not at launch |
| **Home services** | 89.2 on Meta workability, the best Facebook buyer in the entire set, and `RR` = 3. **The easiest buyer to reach is the hardest to convert** |
| **Compliance & Document Ops** | 48.0 on Meta-sellability. Legibility 2, one-line 2, showable 2. Good work, unsellable in an ad |
| **Reporting & Analytics** | Highest-fee *portable* role (74.0 Meta) but `LEGIBLE` = 3. Add once there are case studies to carry it |
| **EA, customer support, data entry** | Somewhere's actual top sellers. All fail a gate — gradeability, async, AI. Volume business, wrong for a new entrant |

## The one number that decides whether any of this works

**Fill rate.** Below 40% the model stops working regardless of which cell you pick
([`scoring/OFFER-MODEL.md`](scoring/OFFER-MODEL.md)).

And before the fill rate matters, two same-week tests:

| # | Test | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Enrich 2,000 companies that posted a remote bookkeeper role → upload as Meta Custom Audience → read the matched size | ~$200, same day |
| **1** | Count real posting volume per vertical on Indeed, OnlineJobs.ph, Upwork and Somewhere's own board | One afternoon, free |

Everything above is `[E]`. Those two tests convert the map from estimate to fact, and they cost
under $500 combined.
