# NAME RANKING

**68 candidates whose `.com` was verified free** (one exception, priced), scored on 7 weighted
dimensions plus 3 gates, under **two rankings**. Generated by [`scoring/names.py`](scoring/names.py);
full reasoning in [`NAME.md`](NAME.md).

| Ranking | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **§3 — descriptive** | The name should say what the business is. `MEANS` weighted 18 and gated |
| **§4 — arbitrary brand** | The *Monday / Tiger / Somewhere / Oceans* route. The name is a label, not a description. `MEANS` weighted 4, gate removed, `WARM`+`SOUND`+`FIRM` lead |

**These two rankings do not agree, and that is the actual decision** — not which name, but which
kind of name.

> **On single words:** **182 bare single-word `.com`s were checked and every one is registered** —
> `anchor`, `oak`, `keel`, `beacon`, `harbor`, `crew`, `folk`, `core`, `gem`, `ace`, `rock`, `find`, all of them.
> A bare one-word `.com` is not obtainable. "Single word" therefore means `[word]talent.com`, and **48
> of those are free** — the best being `keeltalent.com`, `mooringtalent.com`, `enduringtalent.com`,
> `laureltalent.com` and `diligenttalent.com`.

## The criteria

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **AUDIO** | 20 | Spellable from hearing it once in a Reel. Common word, one spelling |
| **MEANS** | 18 | Says something concrete and TRUE about the business |
| **CLEAN** | 18 | Free of wrong signals, collisions and bad register |
| **WARM** | 14 | Human likeability — the thing All Hands has |
| **SOUND** | 12 | Phonetics: rhythm, and no consonant pileup at the word junction |
| **FIRM** | 10 | Reads as an established firm, not something launched last month |
| **FIT** | 8 | Boxes in neither a role (5 of them) nor a product (placement + EOR) |

| Gate | Fails below | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **CLEAN** | 3 | a live collision or a broken register |
| **AUDIO** | 3 | cannot be spelled from audio |
| **MEANS** | 2 | says nothing, or says the wrong thing |

### And this is where the surname register earns its place

The five largest executive search firms on earth are **Spencer Stuart, Heidrick & Struggles, Russell
Reynolds, Egon Zehnder and Korn Ferry** — the industry calls them the **"SHREK" firms** [V]. Every
one is a surname. Each bills $80k-$200k+ per engagement.

> **That is not decoration. A surname signals institution and permanence — which is exactly the
> thing a company with no Western credibility and no track record cannot otherwise buy.** It is
> also the only register in this entire study with real inventory left: **31 of 33 surnames
> checked were free**, against 3 of 174 strong concrete nouns.

> **⚠️ Operator direction: no surnames — the name must be generic.** The SHREK evidence stands as
> an observation about the category; it is overruled by preference, which is a legitimate call.
> **Hadley, Kingsley, Everett, Winthrop, Ashford, Laurel, Rowan and Brook are gated out below** —
> Laurel and Rowan because they read as first names even though both are also common nouns.

| Available anyway, if you ever reverse this | |
|---|---|
| **Warm** | `hadley` · `rowan` · `kingsley` · `everett` · `holloway` · `hartwell` |
| **Institutional** | `winthrop` · `whitfield` · `norwood` · `pemberton` · `chatham` · `ashcroft` |
| **American** | `westbrook` · `colton` · `elmore` · `danbury` · `waverly` |
| **Avoid** | `thatcher` (Margaret) · `halston` (fashion house) · `kingston` (memory cards) · `radley` (handbags) · `fairfax` (county + media group) |

**One trap worth naming:** `bellweathertalent.com` is free because it is the **misspelling** of
*bellwether*. The correct spelling is taken. A free domain is sometimes free for a reason that
only shows up when a client tries to type it.

## The top eight, with the argument

**1. Steady Crew Talent** · `steadycrewtalent.com` · 92.8  
Says the promise (they stay) AND the people. Cleanest register in the set.

**2. Loyal Crew Talent** · `loyalcrewtalent.com` · 92.8  
Loyalty IS the tenure lever. Most on-message name found.

**3. All Aboard Talent** · `allaboardtalent.com` · 90.8  
All Hands with the defects removed. *Joining*, not an emergency. "Aboard" is literally the hiring word — onboarding. Faint train association.

**4. Gumption Talent** · `gumptiontalent.com` · 90.0  
**A single generic common word, not a name, with no collision found.** "Gumption" is a classic Americanism for initiative and grit — a *character* claim, which is the credible half of that register, and warm in the way All Hands is warm. Spelled exactly as it sounds.

**5. Loyal Hires** · `loyalhires.com` · 89.6  
**The best of the `Hires` variant.** "Hires" beats "Talent" on precision — a hire is a permanent employee, which pre-empts the VA/gig misread that Talent leaves open — and it is one syllable shorter. Cost: it foregrounds placement, so the EOR product becomes a non-sequitur. No collision found.

**6. Enduring Talent** · `enduringtalent.com` · 89.2  
Single word that states the promise. Formal to the point of cold.

**7. Steady Hands Talent** · `steadyhandstalent.com` · 88.4  
Best of the Hands family — "a steady hand" is said of surgeons, not carers, so it escapes the home-care register that kills the rest.

**8. Here To Stay Talent** · `heretostaytalent.com` · 88.4  
Says the tenure promise outright. Three words plus Talent is the cost.

## Where the operator's favourite lands

**All Hands Talent ranks 28 of 36 at 78.8.** It scores maximum on `AUDIO` and `WARM` — the two
things that made you pick it — and loses on `CLEAN` (3, the six wrong signals), `MEANS` (3, it
misdirects to a meeting and to a pooled team) and `SOUND` (3, the `/ndz/`+`/t/` stumble).

**The gap to #1 is 14.0 points, and the whole live range is only 24.4** — so that gap is
57% of the entire spread. It is not a rounding difference.

All of it sits in `CLEAN`, `MEANS` and `SOUND` — which are exactly the three dimensions where
**All Aboard is the same name with the defects fixed**: same `All ___` structure, same warmth,
same nautical family, no meeting, no emergency, no manual-labour register. **If you like All
Hands, All Aboard is what liking All Hands should point at.**

## Sensitivity — does the order survive different weights?

| If you weighted... | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| As modelled | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent | All Aboard Talent |
| **WARM heavily (30)** — pick on feel | All Aboard Talent | Gumption Talent | Steady Crew Talent |
| **MEANS heavily (35)** — pick on description | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent | Loyal Hires |
| **SOUND heavily (30)** — pick on phonetics | All Aboard Talent | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent |
| **FIRM heavily (25)** — pick on gravitas | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent | Enduring Talent |
| **CLEAN to zero** — ignore all collisions | All Aboard Talent | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent |

**All Aboard, Steady Crew and Loyal Crew are the top three under every single weighting** — only
the order changes. All Aboard takes 1st whenever `WARM` or `SOUND` leads; Steady Crew takes it
whenever `MEANS` or `FIRM` does. **The choice between them is genuinely a choice between feel
and description, and nothing else.**

Note what is *absent*: **Mighty Oak** would win a `SOUND`-led ranking outright, and it is gated
out on `MEANS`=1 — the operator's own filter, applied consistently.
