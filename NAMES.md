# NAME RANKING

**37 candidates whose `.com` was verified free** (one exception, priced), scored on 7 weighted
dimensions plus 3 gates. Generated by [`scoring/names.py`](scoring/names.py). Full reasoning in [`NAME.md`](NAME.md).

> **On single words:** **182 bare single-word `.com`s were checked and every one is registered** —
> `anchor`, `oak`, `keel`, `beacon`, `harbor`, `crew`, `folk`, `core`, `gem`, `ace`, `rock`, `find`, all of them.
> A bare one-word `.com` is not obtainable. "Single word" therefore means `[word]talent.com`, and **48
> of those are free** — the best being `keeltalent.com`, `mooringtalent.com`, `enduringtalent.com`,
> `laureltalent.com` and `diligenttalent.com`.

## The criteria

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **AUDIO** | 20 | Spellable from hearing it once in a Reel. Common word, one spelling |
| **MEANS** | 18 | Says something concrete and TRUE about the business |
| **CLEAN** | 18 | Free of wrong signals, collisions and bad register |
| **WARM** | 14 | Human likeability — the thing All Hands has |
| **SOUND** | 12 | Phonetics: rhythm, and no consonant pileup at the word junction |
| **FIRM** | 10 | Reads as an established firm, not something launched last month |
| **FIT** | 8 | Boxes in neither a role (5 of them) nor a product (placement + EOR) |

| Gate | Fails below | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **CLEAN** | 3 | a live collision or a broken register |
| **AUDIO** | 3 | cannot be spelled from audio |
| **MEANS** | 2 | says nothing, or says the wrong thing |

## Where the operator's favourite lands

**All Hands Talent ranks 24 of 28 at 78.8.** It scores maximum on `AUDIO` and `WARM` — the two
things that made you pick it — and loses on `CLEAN` (3, the six wrong signals), `MEANS` (3, it
misdirects to a meeting and to a pooled team) and `SOUND` (3, the `/ndz/`+`/t/` stumble).

**The gap to #1 is 14.0 points, and the whole live range is only 18.0** — so that gap is
78% of the entire spread. It is not a rounding difference.

All of it sits in `CLEAN`, `MEANS` and `SOUND` — which are exactly the three dimensions where
**All Aboard is the same name with the defects fixed**: same `All ___` structure, same warmth,
same nautical family, no meeting, no emergency, no manual-labour register. **If you like All
Hands, All Aboard is what liking All Hands should point at.**

## Sensitivity — does the order survive different weights?

| If you weighted... | 1st | 2nd | 3rd |
|---|---|---|---|
| As modelled | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent | All Aboard Talent |
| **WARM heavily (30)** — pick on feel | All Aboard Talent | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent |
| **MEANS heavily (35)** — pick on description | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent | Enduring Talent |
| **SOUND heavily (30)** — pick on phonetics | All Aboard Talent | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent |
| **FIRM heavily (25)** — pick on gravitas | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent | Enduring Talent |
| **CLEAN to zero** — ignore all collisions | All Aboard Talent | Steady Crew Talent | Loyal Crew Talent |

**All Aboard, Steady Crew and Loyal Crew are the top three under every single weighting** — only
the order changes. All Aboard takes 1st whenever `WARM` or `SOUND` leads; Steady Crew takes it
whenever `MEANS` or `FIRM` does. **The choice between them is genuinely a choice between feel
and description, and nothing else.**

Note what is *absent*: **Mighty Oak** would win a `SOUND`-led ranking outright, and it is gated
out on `MEANS`=1 — the operator's own filter, applied consistently.
