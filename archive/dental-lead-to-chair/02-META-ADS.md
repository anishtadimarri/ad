# Meta Ads

## Before you write a single ad

Open **Meta Ad Library**, filter US, and pull every advertiser targeting dentists that has been
running **more than 90 days**. Sustained spend is proof the angle works. Copy the survivors.

Do not invent a hook in a category that already has proven ones.

---

## Targeting

### Campaign structure

```
Campaign: LEAD GEN — Full Arch (Objective: Leads, optimize for conversion event = SLO purchase
                                 if volume allows; else Lead)
│
├── Ad Set 1: BROAD          — no interest layer, let creative filter
├── Ad Set 2: DENTAL INTEREST — interest stack below
├── Ad Set 3: LOOKALIKE      — 1% LAL from SLO buyers (activate at 50+ buyers only)
└── Ad Set 4: RETARGETING    — LP visitors + video 50% viewers, 30 days
```

### Settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Geography | United States. **Split: one-party-consent states vs. all-party-consent states** (see below) |
| Age | 30–65 |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements |
| Optimization | Conversions. Start with Lead, move to Purchase once you have 50+ SLO buys |
| Budget | $100/day to start, $166/day at scale ($5,000/mo) |
| Attribution | 7-day click, 1-day view |

### Interest stack (Ad Set 2)

Dental implants · Dentistry · Nobel Biocare · Straumann · ZimVie · Glidewell Dental ·
Dentrix · Eaglesoft · Open Dental · Dental Economics · DentistryIQ · Dental Town ·
American Academy of Implant Dentistry · Academy of Osseointegration

**Narrow by employer/job title where available:** Dentist · Oral Surgeon · Prosthodontist ·
Periodontist · Dental Practice Owner

### Geographic split — this matters

Ghost calls are the SLO deliverable. Recording consent law forces two versions:

| Ad Set | States | Ad promises |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 38 one-party consent states | "**hear the recording**" |
| **B** | CA, FL, PA, IL, MD, MA, MI, MT, NV, NH, OR, WA | "**read the word-for-word transcript**" |

Same offer, one word different. Do not run the recording promise nationally.

### Exclusions

Dental students · Dental assistants · Dental hygienists · Anyone who purchased the SLO ·
Existing clients

## Creative

### The hook rule

**First three words must name them.** "Full-arch dentists:" filters harder at the impression
level than any audience setting Meta offers.

---

### Ad 1 — The Tape (primary; expect this to win)

**Primary text:**

> Full-arch dentists:
>
> We called 40 implant practices last month pretending to be a $40,000 patient.
>
> 31 of them lost the case in under two minutes.
>
> Not on price. Nobody handled the money question, nobody mentioned financing, and nobody
> called back.
>
> We'll call your office the same way and send you exactly what your front desk said.
>
> Most dentists find it uncomfortable. All of them find it useful.

**Headline:** Hear what your office says when a $40,000 patient calls
**Description:** 12-point scorecard + your leaked case value, in 72 hours
**CTA:** Learn more

**Creative:** 30–45 sec video. Audio waveform on screen, redacted real ghost call audio,
burned-in captions (85% watch muted). Bleep the practice name. End card with the scorecard.

---

### Ad 2 — The No-Show Number

**Primary text:**

> Full-arch dentists: you paid roughly $300 to book that consult.
>
> 35% of them don't show up.
>
> On 25 consults a month, that's 9 people you paid for who never walked in. Nine × $300 =
> $2,700 gone. And the $35,000 case behind each one.
>
> No-shows aren't a patient problem. They're a confirmation-sequence problem, and it's fixable
> in 21 days.
>
> We don't touch your ads. We fix what happens between the booking and the chair.

**Headline:** Your no-show rate is a systems problem
**CTA:** Learn more

**Creative:** Static, dark background, big number "35%" with the math beneath it. Cheapest CPL
of the set; lower quality leads. Good for volume testing.

---

### Ad 3 — The Financing Decline

**Primary text:**

> Full-arch dentists: a patient wants the treatment, applies for financing, gets declined, and
> walks out.
>
> You just lost $35,000 to one lender's algorithm.
>
> Practices running a single lender approve around 50% of full-arch applicants. A properly
> routed waterfall of five lenders gets to 70–80%.
>
> Same patients. Same treatment plans. Two more cases a month.

**Headline:** One lender is costing you $70,000 a month
**CTA:** Learn more

**Creative:** Simple animation of a decline routing through 5 lenders to an approval. Or talking
head. This ad will attract the most sophisticated buyers.

---

### Ad 4 — The Abandoned Pile

**Primary text:**

> Full-arch dentists: what happens to the patient who says "let me think about it"?
>
> For most practices: two follow-up calls, then nothing.
>
> But a full-arch decision takes 3 to 7 months. You quit at week two — and their bone keeps
> resorbing while they wait.
>
> Eleven of those patients are sitting in your system right now. That's $385,000 of pipeline
> nobody is working.

**Headline:** 11 patients said "not yet." Who's following up?
**CTA:** Learn more

---

### Ad 5 — The Anti-Agency Angle

**Primary text:**

> Full-arch dentists: we will never ask you to fire your marketing agency.
>
> Everyone in your inbox wants to replace your ads. We don't do ads.
>
> We work on what happens *after* the lead lands — the no-shows, the financing declines, and
> the "let me think about it" pile.
>
> Your agency looks better. You get more cases. Nobody has to have an awkward conversation.

**Headline:** We don't touch your marketing
**CTA:** Learn more

**Note:** This is the differentiation play. In a category where every advertiser asks the
dentist to doubt their current vendor, being the one who doesn't is genuinely open positioning
— and it turns agencies into a referral channel.

---

### Ad 6 — Testimonial (activate at month 2)

Do not fake this. Film it the day your first client's consults-sat number lands. Expect it to
outperform every ad above by 2–3x, which is why ads go live in week 4 and not week 1.

**Structure:** Owner-dentist on camera, 60 seconds. *"Our no-show rate was X. It's now Y. That's
Z more patients in the chair every month. I didn't change my marketing and I didn't change my
team."*

## Test plan

### Week 1–2: creative test

$100/day, one ad set (BROAD), all 5 ads, let Meta allocate. Kill anything above 2x the best
CPL after 50 impressions per ad minimum.

### Week 3–4: audience test

Winner creative across BROAD vs. DENTAL INTEREST. Add retargeting.

### Week 5+: scale

Winner creative + winner audience, raise budget 20%/day maximum. Add lookalike at 50 SLO buyers.
Refresh creative every 3–4 weeks — this audience is small enough to fatigue.

### Benchmarks to hold yourself to

| Metric | Target | Kill threshold |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | $18–35 | — |
| CTR (link) | >1.2% | <0.6% |
| Cost per lead (calculator completion) | **$45** | >$90 |
| Lead → SLO purchase | **8%** | <3% |
| Cost per SLO buyer | **~$560** | >$900 |
| Cost per booked review call | **~$188** | **>$600 — stop and reassess** |

The last row is the number the whole business hangs on. Everything else is diagnostic.

## Audience capacity

| Monthly spend | Impressions @ $25 CPM | Frequency vs. ~800k audience |
|---|---|---|
| $5,000 | 200,000 | 0.25 — no saturation |
| $15,000 | 600,000 | 0.75 — fine |
| $30,000 | 1.2M | 1.5 — healthy |
| $50,000 | 2.0M | 2.5 — **fatigue begins** |

You can scale to roughly $30,000/month before audience size binds. Delivery capacity will bind
first, at around 8–10 clients.
