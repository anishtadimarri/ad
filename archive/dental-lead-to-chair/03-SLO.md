# The SLO — $297 Full-Arch Leak Audit

**SLO = Self-Liquidating Offer.** A paid front-end priced so that ad spend is largely or fully
recovered before anyone buys the main offer. It does four jobs at once:

1. **Recovers ~55–65% of ad spend**, which cuts effective CAC from ~$2,350 to ~$1,428
2. **Filters** — a dentist who pays $297 is a buyer, not a browser
3. **Demonstrates** competence instead of promising it
4. **Books the review call** with someone who has already given you money

---

## The offer

> ### The Full-Arch Leak Audit — $297
>
> We call your office three times as a prospective $40,000 full-arch patient. You get the
> recordings, a 12-point scorecard, your estimated annual leaked case value, and the complete
> Financing Waterfall Blueprint we install for our clients.
>
> **If we don't find at least $50,000 of annual leaked case value, you keep everything and we
> refund the $297.**

### What they receive (within 72 hours)

| Deliverable | Format |
|---|---|
| 3 ghost patient call recordings | Audio files, private page (transcript instead in all-party-consent states) |
| 12-point leak scorecard | 1-page PDF, scored 0–24 |
| Annual leaked case value calculation | Shown with the arithmetic, using their own numbers |
| **The Financing Waterfall Blueprint** | 14-page PDF: 5-lender stack, credit-tier routing map, application links, 4 presentation scripts |
| 48-hour follow-up test result | Did anyone call the "patient" back? |
| 20-minute review call | Calendly link on the delivery page |

### Why include the Financing Blueprint

The ghost call creates pain. The Blueprint creates goodwill and proves you actually know
something. It's also **a genuine piece of the main offer** — so using it creates appetite for
the rest of the system rather than substituting for it.

A dentist who implements the Blueprint alone gets real results, comes back, and buys. A dentist
who reads it and does nothing has just proven he needs done-for-you.

### The refund guarantee is free

You will always find $50,000 of leaked case value. At 25 consults/month and a 35% no-show rate,
the no-show leak alone is 9 × 12 × 28% × $35,000 = **$1,058,400/year.** The guarantee costs you
nothing and removes all purchase risk.

## Ghost call SOP

### Timing — three calls, deliberately chosen

| Call | When | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tuesday or Wednesday, ~10:00am local | Baseline handling, best-case staffing |
| 2 | Thursday, **4:45pm local** | Rushed handling right before close |
| 3 | Saturday morning | After-hours capture, answering service quality |

Then check at T+48h: **did anyone follow up?** This is usually the most damning finding.

### The script

Play a caller arranging treatment for a parent. This is the most common real full-arch inquiry
and it naturally surfaces the decision-maker question.

> **Opening:**
> "Hi — I'm calling about my dad. His bottom teeth are failing, the dentist said they all need
> to come out. Somebody mentioned you do the thing where they put in implants and do the whole
> arch at once. Can you tell me roughly what that runs?"

**Then, only if they engage — do not force these:**

- "Is there financing for something like that?"
- "How long does the whole process take?"
- "Do you do a free consultation, or is there a charge?"
- "Should I bring him in or can I come first?"

**If asked who you are:** give a real first name and a working mobile number. You need the
48-hour follow-up test to be answerable.

**Never:**
- Never claim to be a patient of record
- Never ask for clinical advice
- Never record in an all-party-consent state — take verbatim notes instead
- Never keep anyone on the phone more than 4 minutes

### Recording consent — the operational rule

| States | Method |
|---|---|
| 38 one-party consent states | Record. Deliver audio |
| **CA, FL, PA, IL, MD, MA, MI, MT, NV, NH, OR, WA** | **Do not record.** Take verbatim notes during the call. Deliver a transcript |

The transcript version is weaker but still lands. A written verbatim of *"we can't discuss
pricing over the phone, let me take a message"* is devastating on the page.

**Get this reviewed by a US attorney with the script in hand before call one.** It's a 45-minute
conversation and it determines your ad targeting geography.

## The 12-point scorecard

Score each 0 / 1 / 2. Maximum 24.

| # | Item | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Answered by a human within 4 rings | Voicemail | 5+ rings or IVR maze | Human, ≤4 rings |
| 2 | Got caller's name and callback number | Neither | One | Both |
| 3 | Handled the price question | Refused | Deflected to "consult" | Gave a range |
| 4 | Gave a specific number or range | No | Vague | Specific $ range |
| 5 | **Mentioned financing unprompted** | No | Only when asked | Yes, unprompted |
| 6 | Offered to book a consult | No | "Someone will call you" | Yes |
| 7 | Offered a **specific** date and time | No | "This week sometime" | Named slot |
| 8 | Asked about the patient's situation | No | One question | Real discovery |
| 9 | Identified the decision maker | No | — | Asked who decides/pays |
| 10 | Confirmed contact info for follow-up | No | Partial | Complete |
| 11 | **Follow-up within 48 hours** | Nothing | One text or voicemail | Call + text |
| 12 | After-hours handling (Saturday) | Dead line | Generic voicemail | Live answer or good triage |

### Interpreting the score

| Score | Verdict on the report |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | **Critical.** You are losing most inbound full-arch opportunities at the phone |
| 9–14 | **Leaking badly.** Booking happens sometimes; money and follow-up are unhandled |
| 15–19 | **Above average.** Fixable gaps, mostly in financing and follow-up |
| 20–24 | **Strong.** Your leak is downstream — nurture and financing declines |

Even a 20+ practice has a leak worth $50,000+, because no-shows and the abandoned pile are
invisible at the phone. Nobody escapes the guarantee condition.

## Leaked case value calculation

Show the arithmetic. A number without visible math reads as invented.

```
INPUTS (from their calculator submission)
  C  = full-arch consults booked per month
  A  = same-day acceptance rate
  V  = average case value

OBSERVED (from ghost calls)
  M  = miss rate — % of the 3 calls where no consult was booked
  F  = financing mentioned unprompted?  (yes/no)
  R  = 48-hour follow-up occurred?      (yes/no)

LEAK 1 — PHONE
  Annual = C × M × 12 × A × V

LEAK 2 — NO-SHOW  (industry baseline 35%; conservative 30%)
  Annual = C × 0.30 × 12 × A × V

LEAK 3 — FINANCING  (single-lender ≈50% approval; waterfall ≈75%)
  Annual = C × (1 − 0.30) × 12 × 0.25 × V × 0.40
           └ consults sat ─┘        └ decline gap ┘  └ recoverable ┘

LEAK 4 — ABANDONED PILE  (recover 10% of non-closers over 6 months)
  Annual = C × (1 − 0.30) × (1 − A) × 12 × 0.10 × V

TOTAL ANNUAL LEAKED CASE VALUE = L1 + L2 + L3 + L4
```

### Worked example — 25 consults, 28% acceptance, $35,000 case, miss rate 2 of 3

| Leak | Calculation | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | 25 × 0.67 × 12 × 0.28 × $35,000 | **$1,969,800** |
| No-show | 25 × 0.30 × 12 × 0.28 × $35,000 | **$882,000** |
| Financing | 25 × 0.70 × 12 × 0.25 × $35,000 × 0.40 | **$735,000** |
| Abandoned pile | 25 × 0.70 × 0.72 × 12 × 0.10 × $35,000 | **$529,200** |
| | | **$4,116,000** |

That number is large enough to be unbelievable, which is a real problem. **On the report, lead
with the recoverable subset — Leaks 2, 3 and 4 only — and present Leak 1 separately as "at the
phone."** Credibility beats magnitude.

Recoverable total in the example: **$2,146,200.** Still enormous. Still true. Present the
monthly figure ($178,850) rather than the annual — it lands better and invites less skepticism.

| T+72h | **Delivery SMS sent** | Automated |

### The delivery SMS — this is the highest-leverage message in the funnel

> Dr. [Last name] — called your office Tuesday 10am and Thursday 4:45pm as a full-arch patient.
> Recordings and scorecard here: [link]
>
> The Thursday call is the one you'll want to hear.

Short. Specific. No pitch. The link does the work. That last line drives the click.

### Delivery page structure

1. Audio player, Call 2 first (the worst one), autoplay off
2. The other two calls beneath
3. 12-point scorecard, visual, with score out of 24
4. **Monthly** recoverable leak figure with the arithmetic shown
5. The 48-hour follow-up result stated plainly
6. Financing Waterfall Blueprint download
7. Single CTA: **"Book your 20-minute review"**

No other links. No navigation. One action.

The SLO nearly doubles the ratio without changing a single thing about the main offer.
