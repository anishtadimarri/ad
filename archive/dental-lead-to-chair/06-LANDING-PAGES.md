# Landing Pages — Copy Decks

Three pages. A deployable HTML build of Page 2 is in
[`landing-pages/slo.html`](landing-pages/slo.html).

```
AD ──► PAGE 1: Leak Calculator (free)  ──► PAGE 2: $297 Audit (checkout)  ──► PAGE 3: Delivery
                                                                                     │
                                                                                     ▼
                                                                            Review call booked
```

**Test both entry paths.** Path A: ad → calculator → SLO offer. Path B: ad → SLO page directly.
Path B has lower volume but far higher intent; path A builds a nurture list. Run both, split
70/30 in favour of A initially.

# Page 1 — The Leak Calculator (free lead magnet)

**Purpose:** capture the lead, produce a number they didn't have, hand them straight to the SLO.
**Conversion target:** 25–35% of visitors complete it.

## Above the fold

**Eyebrow:** For full-arch implant practices

**H1:**
> ## What is your practice losing between the lead and the chair?

**Subhead:**
> Answer 5 questions. See the dollar figure most full-arch practices never calculate — no-shows,
> financing declines, and the patients who said "let me think about it" and were never called
> again.

**Button:** Calculate my leak → *(2 minutes, no email required to see the number)*

**Trust strip beneath the button:**
> We don't sell marketing. We don't touch your ads. We never contact your patients.

That last line does more work than any social proof you could put there — it removes the reflex
suspicion a dentist has toward anything in this category.

## The calculator — 5 questions

| # | Question | Input |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full-arch consults booked per month | Dropdown: <10 · 10–15 · 16–25 · 26–40 · 40+ |
| 2 | Roughly what percent don't show up? | Slider 0–60%, default 30% |
| 3 | Of those who sit, what percent accept same-day? | Slider 0–60%, default 28% |
| 4 | Average full-arch case value | Dropdown: $20–25k · $25–30k · $30–40k · $40–55k · $55k+ |
| 5 | How many financing lenders do you run? | Dropdown: 1 · 2 · 3 · 4+ |

**Show the number before asking for the email.** Give first, ask second — it roughly doubles
completion and the email you get is from someone who has already seen a reason to care.

## The result screen

> ### Your practice is leaking approximately
> # $178,850 / month
> ### in recoverable full-arch revenue

**Broken down:**

| Where | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Patients who book and don't show | $73,500 |
| Financing declines you never re-routed | $61,250 |
| "Let me think about it" — never followed up | $44,100 |

*Based on your numbers: 25 consults/month, 30% no-show, 28% same-day acceptance, $35,000 average
case, single lender. Full arithmetic below.*

**Then, the arithmetic shown openly.** A number without visible math reads as invented.

**CTA block:**
> **That's the estimate. Want to know your actual number?**
>
> We'll call your office three times as a prospective $40,000 full-arch patient and send you the
> recordings, a 12-point scorecard, and your real leak figure. 72 hours.
>
> **[ See the Leak Audit → ]**

**Email capture:** appears here, framed as "send me a copy of these numbers." Fields: name,
practice name, email, mobile. Mobile is required — the delivery SMS is your highest-converting
message.

# Page 2 — The $297 Leak Audit (the SLO)

**Purpose:** take $297 from a qualified dentist.
**Conversion target:** 8% of calculator completions; 2–3% of cold ad traffic on Path B.

## Above the fold

**Eyebrow:** Full-arch implant practices only

**H1:**
> # Hear what your office says when a $40,000 patient calls

**Subhead:**
> We call your practice three times as a prospective full-arch patient. You get the recordings,
> a 12-point scorecard, and the exact dollar figure you're leaking every month.
>
> **72 hours. $297.**

**Button:** Order my Leak Audit — $297

**Risk reversal, directly under the button, boxed:**
> **If we don't find at least $50,000 of annual leaked case value, you keep everything and we
> refund the $297.**

## Section — The proof block

> ### We called 40 implant practices last month pretending to be a $40,000 patient.
> ### 31 of them lost the case in under two minutes.

Not on price. Here's what actually happened:

| What we found | How often |
|---|---|
| Couldn't or wouldn't discuss price at all | 27 of 40 |
| Never mentioned financing | 31 of 40 |
| Never offered a specific appointment time | 22 of 40 |
| **Nobody followed up within 48 hours** | **34 of 40** |
| Saturday call went to a dead line or generic voicemail | 29 of 40 |

*Every practice in that sample was actively running paid ads for full-arch.*

> **They were buying $300 consults and losing them at the front desk for free.**

## Section — What you get

**1. Three recorded calls**
Tuesday morning, Thursday at 4:45pm right before close, and Saturday. Different staff, different
pressure, different results. You'll hear all three.

**2. The 12-point scorecard**
Scored 0–24 across: live answer, price handling, financing mention, specific appointment offered,
discovery, decision-maker identification, 48-hour follow-up, after-hours capture.

**3. Your leak figure**
The real number, with the arithmetic shown, using your consult volume and your case value. Not a
range. Not an industry average.

**4. The Financing Waterfall Blueprint** *(this is the part practices actually keep)*
The complete 14-page document we install for clients: the 5-lender stack, the credit-tier routing
map, application links, and the 4 presentation scripts. Single-lender practices approve around
50% of full-arch applicants. A properly routed waterfall reaches 70–80%.

> **Yours to keep and implement whether or not you ever speak to us again.**

**5. A 20-minute review call**
Optional. We walk the recordings with you. No pitch unless you ask for one.

## Section — What this is not

- ❌ Not a marketing audit. We don't look at your ads, your website, or your SEO.
- ❌ Not a sales training pitch. We don't coach treatment coordinators.
- ❌ Not a lead-gen offer. We will never ask you to fire your agency.

> We work on one thing: what happens between a lead arriving and a patient in your chair.

## Section — Who this is for

**Order it if:** you do 15+ full-arch consults/month, average case value $25,000+, and you're
spending real money on patient acquisition.

**Don't order it if:** you do fewer than 15 full-arch consults/month. There isn't enough volume
for the findings to be worth $297, and we'll tell you that rather than take your money.

*(This paragraph raises conversion. Explicit disqualification reads as confidence.)*

## Order form

Fields: Practice name · Your name · **Practice phone** (the number we'll call) · Your mobile ·
Email · Full-arch consults per month (dropdown) · Average case value (dropdown) · Card

**Below the button:**
> One-time $297. No subscription. Nothing recurring. Delivered in 72 hours.
>
> **Don't warn your team — it defeats the purpose.**

That last line is the best sentence on the page. It creates complicity, builds anticipation, and
protects the integrity of your own deliverable.

## FAQ

**Is this legal?** Yes. We identify as a prospective patient making a genuine inquiry, which is
standard mystery-shopping practice. In the twelve states requiring all-party consent to record, we
deliver a word-for-word transcript rather than audio.

**Will my staff know?** Not unless you tell them. Please don't.

**What if my score is bad?** Most are. The scorecard averages 14 of 24 across the practices we've
audited. The point isn't the grade, it's the dollar figure attached to it.

**Do you contact my patients?** Never. We call your front desk. That's the entire interaction.

**What happens after?** You get a 20-minute review call if you want it. If you'd rather just take
the report and the Blueprint and go implement it yourself, that's a completely fine outcome.

# Page 3 — Delivery Page (private, per-client URL)

**Purpose:** land the pain, then convert to a booked review call.
**Target:** 85% of audit buyers book the call.

## Structure — order matters

**H1:** Your Leak Audit — [Practice Name]

**1. The worst call, first.** Audio player, Call 2 (Thursday 4:45pm) at the top. Autoplay off.
Label it plainly: *"Start here. This is the one you'll want to hear."*

**2. The other two calls** beneath, labelled with day and time.

**3. The scorecard**, visual, score out of 24, with the band interpretation.

**4. The 48-hour follow-up result**, stated in one line with no softening:
> *We gave your office a working mobile number on all three calls. Nobody called it.*

**5. Your monthly leak figure** with the arithmetic shown. Monthly, not annual — annual figures
in the millions trigger disbelief and cost you the sale.

**6. The Financing Waterfall Blueprint** — download button, no gate.

**7. Single CTA:**
> **Book your 20-minute review** — we'll walk the recordings together and I'll show you what we'd
> change. [Calendly embed]

**No navigation. No other links. One action.**

# Design notes

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Layout | Single column, max 680px, generous line height |
| Fonts | System stack. No webfonts |
| Colour | Near-black on off-white. One accent for CTAs. Nothing clinical-blue — every dental vendor uses it |
| Images | None on the SLO page except the scorecard sample. Text and the audio player carry it |
| Mobile | Assume 70%+ mobile. Dentists read email between patients on a phone |
| Load | Under 1.5s. No sliders, no popups, no chat widget, no exit-intent |
| Forms | One column. Never split name into two fields |
| Social proof | **Leave it out until real.** Fake or generic testimonials are worse than none with this buyer |
