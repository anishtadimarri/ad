## 2. Five patterns, and four of them contradict what I told you last time

### 2.1 The category's CTA is a *person*, not a call

| Competitor | CTA |
|---|---|
| Genius | **"See Pre-vetted Candidates"** |
| GrowthAssistant | **"Find My Match"** |
| Athena | **"Find your EA match"** |
| Vidchops | **"Yes! Give Me An Editor Now"** |
| Somewhere / Athyna / Hireframe | "Start Hiring" |

**Not one of them asks for a call in the button.** The call exists — Somewhere, GrowthAssistant and
Vidchops all route to one — but it is never the promise. **The promise is a person.**

> The buyer's question is not *"should I talk to this company?"* It is *"who would I get?"*

### 2.2 Nobody gates. Zero of ten

Not one competitor puts a qualifying question with a real *no* in front of the calendar.
**GrowthAssistant asks one field** — *"tell us about your company"* — and Genius advertises
*"takes 1 minute"* and *"no commitment"*. The category competes on **removing** friction.

I recommended a qualifying gate last time and ranked it first. **Ten out of ten operating
companies disagree.** That is not proof they are right — none of them is a solo unknown
operator, and friction is exactly how an unknown compensates for having no logos. But a
unanimous category is evidence, and §5 prices it rather than dismissing it.

### 2.3 The bench is the lead magnet, and it is on the *homepage*

| Competitor | Named profiles on the demand page | With |
|---|---|---|
| **Athyna** | **40+** | photo, location, role, **hourly rate**, skills |
| **Somewhere** | **9** | photo, country flag, **prior employer** |
| **Genius** | **9** | photo, specialisation, **monthly salary** |
| **GrowthAssistant** | **4** | accomplishments and tool proficiency |
| Hireframe · Athena · Vidpros · Vidchops | 0 | testimonials or nothing |

**Four of the eight live competitors put named, priced humans on the page before any form.**
The four that do not are the two subscription video shops and the two that sell a *service*
rather than a *person* — which is the distinction that matters, because **we sell a person.**

### 2.4 Two pricing models, and the closest competitor uses the one our constraint forbids

| Model | Who | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly retainer** | GrowthAssistant · Hireframe · Vidpros · Vidchops · Athena · Oceans | $1,000–4,000/mo |
| **One-time placement fee** | Somewhere · Genius | 25–35% of first-year |

**GrowthAssistant is the closest competitor in the study** — offshore *marketing and creative*
talent, sold to *DTC/e-comm, agencies and SaaS*, placing *paid social, video editors, designers,
email, SEO, data* — which is our ICP list and our role list. **And they charge $3,500/month.**

§6 does the arithmetic on copying that, and the answer is that **it breaks the 30-day constraint**
— $1,795/month of gross profit cannot clear 1.5:1 against any CAC in this study except two.

**"80% less" appears verbatim in two of eight headlines** — Somewhere and Genius. It is
the category's default claim, and [`MODEL-V2.md`](../MODEL-V2.md) §8 already decided not to
compete there. **This confirms that decision rather than challenging it:** entering a category
on the leader's own axis, with no track record, is the losing side of that fight.

The two most interesting headlines are the ones that do *not* mention cost. **Athena's "The relief**
**is immediate. The ROI is real."** sells a feeling, and **GrowthAssistant's "Built Into Your Team"**
sells integration. Both are available to us. *"80% less"* is not.

## 6. The pricing test — can we copy GrowthAssistant?

They are the closest competitor in the study and they charge **$3,500/month**. Our model is
a **one-time 30% fee**. This matters to the funnel because it sets the CAC budget.

| | Monthly retainer | One-time placement fee |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **$3,500/mo** (GrowthAssistant's number) | ~$6,000 one-time |
| Cost of the seat | $1,650 salary + $55 admin | — |
| **Gross profit inside 30 days** | **$1,795** | **$4,148** |
| Max CAC at 1.5:1 | **$1,197** | **$2,765** |
| Funnels that clear it | **6 of 10** | **9 of 10** |

> **The monthly model fails the operator's own 30-day constraint at almost any CAC.**
> $1,795 of month-one gross profit needs CAC under **$1,197**, and only
> 6 of the ten funnels get there.

That is not an argument that monthly is a worse business — over a 30-month seat it is worth
far more, and it is why every subscription competitor in the study prices that way. It is an
argument that **monthly is unaffordable to *acquire* on paid media until something else funds
the gap.** The one-time fee is what makes Meta viable at all, and Somewhere and Genius — the
two placement-fee players — are also the two with the largest published scale.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Keep the one-time fee as the acquisition offer** | It is the only thing that clears 1.5:1 on cold paid traffic |
| **Sell the monthly line as the attachment** | EOR at $477/employee/month, exactly as [`LTGP.md`](LTGP.md) models it. Same revenue shape as GrowthAssistant, acquired on a fee that pays for the click |
| **Do not match $3,500/mo** | It is a competitor's *whole* price against our *attachment* price. Matching it means competing on the axis where they have logos, a published price and a 92% first-match claim |

## 7. The recommended funnel

**Role-select quiz → matched profiles → call** at **90.2** and **See the bench — 3–6 graded people, teased then unlocked** at **89.9**.
They are **0.3 apart, which this model cannot resolve** — and they should not be
resolved, because they are the same funnel seen from two ends:

> **The bench is the offer. The quiz is how you route to it.**

The quiz asks role, ICP and size in three taps; the bench is what those taps unlock. Run
either alone and you lose something: a quiz with nothing behind it is a form, and a bench
with no routing shows an e-comm owner a bookkeeper. **The numbers below are the hybrid.**

```
  AD        qualifies on ICP + size · names the roles as a menu
            one campaign · one ad set · 4 creatives
     |
     v
  PAGE      3 graded people, FACE UP, above the fold
            name · country · the test they passed · the number they hit
     |
     v
  QUIZ      3 taps: which seat · your size · when
            "Show me who I'd get"                    <-- THE PIXEL EVENT
     |
     v
  PROFILES  the matched bench for that role, each with the actual work
            sample and the measured number. Email to save or share it.
            Calendar embedded beside them
     |
     v
  CALL      45 min. They have already seen who they would get
     |
     v
  SIGNED    one-time fee. EOR offered at signature, not before
```

| Step | Number | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Spend | $5,000/mo | the size [`FUNNEL.md`](FUNNEL.md) argued for test one |
| Landing page views | 3,009 | [E] blended CPM/CTR, 15% pre-paint bounce |
| **Quiz completed** — the pixel event | **211** | **49/week** against Meta's 50. The highest of any high-intent event here |
| Cost per completion | $24 | **below** the verified $30–80 B2B CPL band [V] — a quiz is a smaller ask than a form |
| Calls booked | 63 | [?] 30% of completions book |
| Calls held | 40 | [?] |
| Signed | 12.5 | [?] the softest number in the model |
| **Placements** | **9.6** | 76% fill |
| Founder hours | 47/mo | against ~60 available |
| **CAC** | **$810** | spend + founder time ÷ placements |
| **30-day** | **5.12:1** | against the 1.5:1 constraint |
| **Lifetime** | **13.67:1** |  |

### What this costs you before the first ad

> **Two graded people per launch role.** Four creatives means two roles, so **four humans
> sourced, tested and written up.** That is the entry price, and it is the one thing on the
> list that cannot be bought or shortcut.

It is also work the business needs regardless — [`MINIMUM.md`](../MINIMUM.md) §6 already said
the proof block *is* the business. This funnel makes that block the whole page.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why not the gate** | Zero of ten competitors gates, and it ranked 7 of 10 here. An unknown operator adding friction in a category built on removing it is the wrong trade — **show them a person instead of interviewing them** |
| **Why the bench also qualifies** | Someone who unlocks profiles for a $1,650/month editor has told you their budget by clicking. The sizing question on the unlock form does the rest |
| **Why it satisfies the discovery problem** | Every unlock carries a **role**, and every ad carries an **ICP**. Four weeks of unlocks is a demand map you currently do not have, bought with the same money that buys placements |
| **Where it is weakest** | `GENERIC` 7 of 10 — each role needs its own bench, so adding a role is not free the way adding a calculator label is. **This is the real cost of the recommendation** and it is why two roles, not three |

## 8. The Meta ads

Four creatives, one ad set — because at **49 events a week** against Meta's 50, splitting into separate ad sets
means no ad set ever leaves the learning phase, where CPA runs **20–50% higher** [V].

| Slot | ICP | Role | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | E-comm, $10k+/mo ads | Performance video | *"Running $10k+ a month in Meta ads and shipping 3 new cuts?"* |
| **2** | E-comm, $10k+/mo ads | Paid media | *"Your agency charges 15% of spend. Here is what a full-time buyer costs."* |
| **3** | Agencies, 5–30 staff | Performance video | *"Your editor is the bottleneck on every retainer you have."* |
| **4** | Agencies, 5–30 staff | Paid media | *"You are turning down retainers because you cannot staff them."* |

**The creative shape**, and it is taken from what works in the category rather than invented:

```
  1. HOOK     the ICP + size question they answer yes to
  2. FACE     a real graded person, named, with the number they hit
              <- this is the category's actual mechanic. Genius, Athyna,
                 Somewhere and GrowthAssistant all lead with people
  3. TURN     the volume problem in their units
  4. MENU     the other roles, named, one line
  5. ASK      "See who you'd get"   <- not "book a call"
```

| | |
|---|---|
| **The ask is a person, never a call** | Zero of ten competitors puts a call in the button. **"See who you'd get"** is the category's grammar |
| **Never "80% less"** | Two of eight headlines already say it verbatim. It is the leader's axis and we have no track record to win it with |
| **No employment language, in any of the four** | No *apply*, no *join*, no *hiring now*, and the bench page must read as a **supplier catalogue for buyers**, not a job board. One reviewer tick costs 10–29% on CAC ([`SUPPLY-DEMAND.md`](../SUPPLY-DEMAND.md)) |
| **The face in the ad must be the face on the page** | Message match is the largest silent conversion killer, and here it is literal — the same person, the same number |
