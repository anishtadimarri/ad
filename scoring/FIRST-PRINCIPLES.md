# First Principles — Verticals, Skills, and the Map

Three criteria sets scored **independently**, then combined. Earlier models mixed buyer
properties with work properties into one number, which is why the answer moved every time a
new consideration was introduced.

| | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Vertical** | Properties of the buyer — true whatever role you place |
| **Skill** | Properties of the work — true wherever you place it |
| **Interaction** | Only exists at the intersection: felt pain, existing combo supply, stack fit |

**Pair score = 35% vertical + 35% skill + 30% interaction.**

## 3. Interaction criteria

| Dim | Wt | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| `PAIN` | **40** | Is this skill a top-3 felt pain in THIS vertical? |
| `COMBO` | **35** | Existing offshore supply doing THIS skill FOR THIS vertical |
| `FIT` | **25** | Does the vertical's stack make this skill gradeable HERE? |

The interaction is what the earlier single-score models kept losing. A skill can be
excellent and a vertical can be excellent while the *pair* is weak — because the buyer does
not feel that particular pain, or no one offshore has done that exact combination before.

## 5. Picking winners separately does not work

The top three verticals have **different** best skills:

| Vertical | Best skill | 2nd |
|---|---|---|
| E-comm / DTC | **Bookkeeping / reconciliation** (87.6) | Paid Media Operations (84.9) |
| MSP / IT services | **AR & Collections** (88.7) | AP & Invoice Processing (85.3) |
| Marketing agency | **Reporting & Analytics (BI)** (83.4) | Paid Media Operations (82.0) |

So a naive top-3 × top-3 grid contains weak cells — e-comm's AR is only 74.0 while MSP's
is 88.7. **The right optimisation is to maximise the WORST cell**, because one bench has to
serve every cell in the grid.

| # | Verticals | Skills | Worst cell | Mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E-comm + Marketing agency + MSP | AR & Collections + AP & Invoice Processing + Reporting & Analytics | **72.8** | 80.0 |
| 2 | E-comm + PI law firm + Medical practice | AR & Collections + Bookkeeping / reconciliation + Compliance & Document Ops | **71.6** | 77.8 |
| 3 | E-comm + Staffing agency + Freight | AR & Collections + AP & Invoice Processing + Compliance & Document Ops | **71.5** | 78.7 |
| 4 | Marketing agency + Staffing agency + MSP | AR & Collections + AP & Invoice Processing + Reporting & Analytics | **71.2** | 79.8 |
| 5 | E-comm + Staffing agency + MSP | AR & Collections + AP & Invoice Processing + Reporting & Analytics | **71.2** | 78.2 |

## 6. THE ANSWER

### Verticals — E-comm / DTC · Marketing agency · MSP / IT services

### Skills — AR & Collections · AP & Invoice Processing · Reporting & Analytics (BI)

| | E-comm / DTC | Marketing agency | MSP / IT services |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AR & Collections** | **74.0** | **80.8** | **88.7** |
| **AP & Invoice Processing** | **79.3** | **76.5** | **85.3** |
| **Reporting & Analytics (BI)** | **72.8** | **83.4** | **78.8** |

**Worst cell 72.8 · mean 80.0 · spread 16.0.** Optimising the grid rather
than picking winners separately lifts the worst cell from 68.9 to 72.8 and cuts the spread from 19.9 to 16.0.

**All three skills are the transactional finance layer plus BI — not the senior close.**
Ledger & Close scores 4th on skill and carries the highest fee, but it is the least
portable, so it belongs as a per-vertical add-on rather than in the shared grid.

**Read the grid by column, not by row.** Each vertical has a natural entry skill:

| Vertical | Lead with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **E-comm / DTC** | AP & Invoice Processing (79.3) | highest existing offshore supply, zero switch cost, fastest to fill |
| **Marketing agency** | Reporting & Analytics (BI) (83.4) | highest fee of the three, spans finance and marketing, founder can grade it |
| **MSP / IT services** | AR & Collections (88.7) | billing volume is the felt pain and cash is the language owners speak |
