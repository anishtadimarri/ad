# The Meta Funnel — D2C Owner Buying a Bookkeeper

Every step with the arithmetic shown. **Steps 1–5 are benchmarked against verified
Meta CPL data. Steps 6–9 are assumptions** — no data exists until the first campaign runs.

Constants: **$6,600 fee** (30% of a $22,000 placed salary) · **62% gross
margin** · **85% fill rate** · **$60** of your time per call held.

---

## The nine steps, walked once — realistic middle case

*1% lookalike audience, calendar booking on the landing page, $5,000 spend.*

| | Step | Value | Note |

| 1 | Ad spend | $5,000 | the test budget |
| 2 | Impressions | 312,500 | $16 CPM — US B2B, FB + IG feed and Reels blended [E] |
| 3 | Clicks | 3,438 | 1.1% CTR on a lookalike. Cold broad runs 0.6–0.9% [E] |
|  | → **CPC** | **$1.45** |  |

| 5 | **Calls booked** | **58** | **2%** LP → booking. Booking a call is a big ask; a form fill would convert ~5% but hold far worse |

| 6 | Calls **held** | 38 | 65% show rate on a self-booked slot [?] |

**Gross profit $4,092 ÷ CAC $1,089 = 3.76:1 on 30 days.**

## Form fill vs calendar booking

I expected this to be a clear win for the calendar booking. It is not.

| | Form fill, then chase | Calendar booking on LP |
|---|---|---|
| Leads from $5,000 | 172 | 58 |
| **CPL** | **$29** | **$86** |
| Show / hold rate | 25% | 65% |
| Calls held | 43.0 | 38.0 |
| Cost per held call | $176 | $192 |
| Placements | 7.6 | 6.7 |
| **CAC** | **$1,002** | **$1,089** |
| **30-day ratio** | **4.08:1** | **3.76:1** |

**The form fill wins CPL by 2.9x — and the two flows land within
9% of each other on CAC.** I set this section up expecting the calendar booking to
win. It does not. The higher show rate is almost exactly cancelled by the lower page
conversion, so **CAC does not decide this** and it should be chosen on other grounds:

| Choose | When |
|---|---|
| **Form fill** | You want volume of conversations to learn from fast, and you have the time to chase. More total held calls (43 vs 38) at a slightly lower CAC |
| **Calendar booking** | You want fewer, better conversations. The person who books a slot has already decided to talk, which should show up in a higher deposit rate — an effect this model does not credit it for |

The general principle still holds — **optimise CAC, not the number in the ad dashboard** — it just does not separate these two options.

## By audience layer

| Audience | CPM | CTR | CPL | Cost/held call | **CAC** | **30-day** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Intent seed (job posters)** | $22 | 2.0% | $65 | $160 | **$652** | **6.28:1** |
| **1% Lookalike** | $16 | 1.1% | $86 | $192 | **$1,089** | **3.76:1** |
| **Broad / Advantage+** | $14 | 0.8% | $103 | $218 | **$1,427** | **2.87:1** |

The intent seed carries the **highest CPM** — small audience, thin auction — and still
delivers the **lowest CAC**, because a mid-search buyer converts better at the deposit step.
**That is the entire commercial argument for the scraping pipeline.**

## What $5,000 actually buys

| | |
|---|---|
| Calls booked | 58 |
| Calls held | 38 |
| Deposits | 7.9 |
| **Placements** | **6.7** |
| Revenue | $44,110 |
| Gross profit | $27,348 |
| Spend + your time | $7,279 |
| **Net** | **$20,069** |

**What $5,000 does and does not measure:**

| Measurable at $5,000? | |
|---|---|
| ✅ CPL | 58 bookings is plenty |
| ✅ Cost per held call | 38 held calls is a solid read |
| ⚠️ Deposit rate | 7.9 deposits off 38 calls gives roughly ±10 percentage points. A usable first signal, not a settled number |
| ❌ Fill rate | 7.9 searches cannot measure an 85% assumption |
| ❌ Attrition, replacement rate, repeat rate | months away |

**$5,000 is the right size for test one.** It answers the two questions that gate
everything else — can you buy a held call, and at what price — and gives a first read on
the deposit rate. Do not spend more until those three numbers exist.

## Where it breaks

Baseline: **$1,089 CAC · 3.76:1**

| If this happens | CAC | Clears 1.5:1? |
|---|---|---|
| CTR halves to 0.55% | $1,837 | ✅ 2.23:1 |
| LP books at 1% not 2% | $1,837 | ✅ 2.23:1 |
| Show rate 40% not 65% | $1,557 | ✅ 2.63:1 |
| Deposit rate 8% not 18% | $2,451 | ✅ 1.67:1 |
| Fill rate 50% not 85% | $1,852 | ✅ 2.21:1 |
| CPM $30 not $16 | $1,744 | ✅ 2.35:1 |

**The deposit rate is the fragile one.** Everything else can move a long way and still
clear. Halve the deposit rate and the ratio halves with it — which is why the $500
deposit is the single most important thing to test on the first ten calls.
