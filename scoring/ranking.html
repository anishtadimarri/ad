Where this model is weak

Scores are judgment, not measurement. Around 2,500 cells were assigned from research and reasoning. Buyer remote-readiness in particular is an informed guess for most clusters.
Dimensions correlate. Gradeability and trainability move together, as do volume and reachability. Clusters strong on a correlated pair get inflated.
Fourteen dimensions capped at 5 compress toward the mean. The real range is 49–79 out of a theoretical 0–100.
No interaction terms. High volume combined with low reachability should penalise more than additively. It doesn't.
Recruiter's cyclicality is under-weighted. Recruiting demand is among the most hiring-cycle-sensitive functions in the economy. Retention scored 3; a case exists for 2.
